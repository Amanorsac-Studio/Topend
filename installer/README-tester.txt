TOP END 1.0.0 - TEST BUILD
Amanorsac Studio · amanorsac.studio

WHAT THIS IS
A test build of Top End, a guitar amp, pedalboard and looper for the stage. It is
not the released product. It needs the test key you were sent, and it stops
working when that key expires.

INSTALLING
Windows   Run TopEnd-1.0.0-Tester-Windows.exe and follow the installer.
macOS     Open TopEnd-1.0.0-Tester-macOS.pkg and follow the installer.

YOUR TEST KEY
The first time you open Top End it asks for the key. Paste the whole key, which
is one long line of letters and numbers with a full stop in the middle, and
choose Unlock. Until you do there is no sound. The key is remembered, so you
are asked once. The header shows how many days are left. When the key expires
Top End stops until you enter a new one; ask hello@amanorsac.studio.
The key checks itself on your computer. It never connects to the internet.

WHAT GETS INSTALLED
  Standalone  C:\Program Files\Amanorsac Studio\Top End\
              /Applications/Top End.app
  VST3        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\Top End.vst3
              /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Top End.vst3
  Audio Unit  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Top End.component
  Stem separation (optional, about 170 MB)
              C:\Program Files\Amanorsac Studio\Top End\Models\
              /Library/Application Support/Amanorsac Studio/Top End/Models/
The installer shows these paths and lets you add a second location if you
keep your plug-ins somewhere else.

YOUR PRESETS AND SETTINGS
  Windows   Documents\Amanorsac Studio\Top End\
  macOS     ~/Documents/Amanorsac Studio/Top End/
Uninstalling does not delete these.

SENDING YOUR PRESETS BACK
We would like the presets you make. In Top End, open Library and choose Share
presets, pick the ones to send, and add your name if you want it credited.
They are copied to:
  Windows   Documents\Amanorsac Studio\Top End\Beta Presets\
  macOS     ~/Documents/Amanorsac Studio/Top End/Beta Presets/
Send the files from that folder to hello@amanorsac.studio, or attach them to a
"Preset submission" issue on the beta's GitHub page. Nothing is sent for you.
By sending a preset you agree that Amanorsac Studio may include it, with your
credit if you gave one, in a future version of Top End.

TELLING US WHAT YOU FIND
Write to hello@amanorsac.studio. Say what you did, what you expected and what
happened, and which computer and audio interface you use. Nothing is sent from
Top End for you: it collects nothing and has no usage reporting.

IF YOUR DAW DOES NOT SEE IT
1. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW's preferences.
2. Check the folder above is in your DAW's plug-in scan paths.
3. Restart the DAW.
Still missing? hello@amanorsac.studio

UNINSTALLING
  Windows   Settings > Apps > Top End > Uninstall
  macOS     Run Uninstall Top End.command, or delete the files listed above.
