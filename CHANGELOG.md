# Top End — what changed

## 1.0.0 (beta)

The first version, for testing.

**The rig**
- Six guitar amps, each with Clean, Crunch and Lead channels: Plexi 68, Black Panel, Tweed 57, Top Boost 30, Modern Hi-Gain and Solid Clean.
- 1×12, 2×12 and 4×12 cabinets with microphone choice and placement, and your own impulse responses.
- A pedalboard you build yourself, in any order, before or after the amp: Noise Gate, Clear Drive, Tube Drive, Fuzz, Treble Boost, Studio Comp, FET Squeeze, Guitar EQ, Studio EQ, Wah, Auto Wah, Octave Down, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, Tremolo, Tape Echo, Digital Delay, Room and Hall.
- Pedals sound one after another, as on a real board.
- Bypass All, on a button, a footswitch and the X key, to hear the guitar as it is.
- The noise gate follows the noise your input actually has. A hum filter (50 or 60 Hz) is in Settings › Audio › Noise.

**Presets and sets**
- Presets, and sets that step through them on stage, with footswitches A to F for the scenes. Changing preset never cuts the sound.
- An edit is kept when you change preset, and kept if Top End closes. When you close it, Top End asks whether to save each edit to the preset or to the set only, or to discard it.
- Library › Share presets copies the presets you choose into a folder, ready to send back to us.

**Looper and click**
- A four-layer looper with overdub and undo.
- REC waits for your first note and the loop starts on that bar.
- Loop length: free, or 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars. A set length closes the loop by itself.
- The loop keeps to the click: it starts on the click's bar, and PLAY after STOP comes back in on the next "1".
- Time signatures from 2/4 to 12/8. The "1" of the bar is an octave higher than the other beats.
- The click can go to its own output pair, so the audience does not hear it.

**Learn**
- Import a song or a video and Top End finds the chords, the key and the beat. Chords are shown per bar or per beat, coloured by their job in the key.
- Stem separation on your own computer: drums, bass, vocals, and guitar with keys. Mute the guitar and play along.
- Slow a song down without changing its pitch, change its key, loop any section, count in and play with a click.
- Song timing: set where the song starts and ends, mark a free-time intro and tempo changes, and drag the audio under the beat lines.
- The fretboard shows what you play, at one position.
- A YouTube browser with the same speed and loop controls.

**Cover**
- Play along to a song through the whole rig, record yourself over it, punch in on a section, and export the mix, your guitar and your clean DI as WAV files.

**Audio**
- Every audio interface on every driver in one list, refreshed when one is plugged in or out.
- The input channel, the output pair, mono or stereo, and a separate pair for the click.
- Lowest safe latency picks your interface's own driver, and says so when another program is using the interface.

**Formats**
- Windows: standalone application and VST3.
- macOS: standalone application, VST3 and Audio Unit.
