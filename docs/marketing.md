# Top End — marketing notes

Top End 1.0.0 is in **beta**. "Top End" is a working title and has not been cleared as
a trade mark: use it only in material for testers until the studio confirms the
name. Every line below describes something the beta does today.

## In one line

A guitar amp, pedalboard and looper for the stage, with a tab that teaches you the song.

## Short description (store card, 2 sentences)

Top End is a guitar rig for the stage and the practice room: amps, cabinets and a
pedalboard you build yourself, a looper that keeps time with the click, and
presets and sets that change without a gap. Its Learn tab finds a song's chords,
key and beat, separates it into stems on your own computer, and lets you record
yourself playing over it.

## Description (product page)

**A rig you build.** Six guitar amps, each with Clean, Crunch and Lead channels: Plexi 68, Black Panel, Tweed 57, Top Boost 30, Modern Hi-Gain and Solid Clean. 1×12, 2×12 and 4×12 cabinets with microphone choice and placement, and your own impulse responses. A pedalboard in any order, before
or after the amp: Noise Gate, Clear Drive, Tube Drive, Fuzz, Treble Boost, Studio Comp, FET Squeeze, Guitar EQ, Studio EQ, Wah, Auto Wah, Octave Down, Chorus, Flanger, Phaser, Tremolo, Tape Echo, Digital Delay, Room and Hall. Pedals sound one after another, as on a real
board, and Bypass All lets you hear your guitar as it is.

**Made for the stage.** Presets, and sets that step through them with footswitches
A to F. Changing preset never cuts the sound. An edit you have not saved is kept
when you change preset, and Top End asks what to do with it when you close.

**A looper that keeps time.** Press REC and the loop starts on the bar where you
first play. Choose a length of 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars, or let it run free. The loop
keeps to the click, and when you stop and start it again it comes back in on the
"1". Time signatures from 2/4 to 12/8, with the "1" an octave higher than the
other beats, and the click on its own output so the audience does not hear it.

**Learn the song.** Import a song or a video: Top End finds the chords, the key and
the beat, and shows the chords per bar or per beat, coloured by their job in the
key. Separate it into stems on your own computer, with no internet connection;
guitar and keys share one stem. Mute the guitar and play along. Slow it down without changing its pitch,
change its key, loop a section, and count in with a click. When a song starts
slowly and comes into tempo, mark the free-time intro and set the tempo where it
begins. The fretboard shows what you play, on a six-string neck.

**Play it back.** The Cover tab plays the song through your audio interface and
records you over it with the whole rig. Punch in on one section, keep every take,
and export the mix, your guitar and a clean DI as WAV files.

**Your interface, your way.** Every audio interface on every driver in one list,
the input channel, the output pair, mono or stereo, and the lowest safe latency
in one button. The noise gate follows the noise your input actually has, and a
hum filter removes mains hum.

**Nothing leaves your computer.** Top End has no account and makes no network
requests. Your presets, songs and recordings stay in Documents.

## Features (bullet list)

- Six guitar amps, each with Clean, Crunch and Lead channels: Plexi 68, Black Panel, Tweed 57, Top Boost 30, Modern Hi-Gain and Solid Clean
- 1×12, 2×12 and 4×12 cabinets with microphone choice and placement, and your own impulse responses
- Pedalboard in any order, before or after the amp; Bypass All
- Presets and sets; footswitches A to F; changing preset never cuts the sound
- Unsaved edits kept; on closing, save to the preset, to the set only, or discard
- Looper: four layers, overdub and undo; starts on your first note; 1 to 16 bars; keeps to the click
- Click: time signatures 2/4 to 12/8, accented "1", its own output pair
- Learn: chords, key and beat; stem separation on your computer; speed, pitch, A–B loop, count-in
- Song timing editor: start and end, free-time intro, tempo changes
- Cover: record over a song, punch in, takes, export mix, guitar and DI
- Audio: every interface and driver, channels, mono or stereo, latency shown in milliseconds
- Noise gate that follows your input's noise floor; 50 or 60 Hz hum filter
- Standalone application, VST3 and Audio Unit

## Words we use, and words we do not

- Say what it does: "finds the chords", "separates the song", "keeps time with the click".
- British spelling: licence, colour, behaviour.
- No exclamation marks, no "Oops", no emoji.
- No numbers we have not measured, no comparisons with other products, no testimonials.
- Not "AI-powered": the stem separation runs a model on your computer; say that.
